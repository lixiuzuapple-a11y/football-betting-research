## 1. Executive result

The five-day prospective dataset contains real same-round Sporttery-vs-BetExplorer residuals, including positive `proxy_implied_edge` observations.

However, the pattern does **not** pass the pre-registered source-quality and concentration gates required to interpret those residuals as a stable B01 pricing mechanism.

The decisive findings are:

1. **Variant dependence is material.**
   - all 57 matched fixture-selection pairs were observed under multiple BetExplorer declared variants;
   - 10/57 have opposite-sign median residuals across variants;
   - 14 fixture-selection pairs ever show positive residual;
   - only **2/14** show positive residual under every variant in which they were observed;
   - therefore **12/14 positive fixture-selection pairs are variant-dependent**.

2. **Large residuals are highly concentrated in a few fixtures.**
   - at `>=5%`, the top two fixtures contribute **87.2%** of positive rows;
   - at `>=10%`, the top two fixtures contribute **93.3%** of positive rows.

3. **Forward-reference movement provides some supporting direction, but not enough to rescue the mechanism.**
   - many positive residual observations are unchanged at the next same-variant observation;
   - at longer horizons, reductions outnumber increases among uncensored observations;
   - this is compatible with some real relative-price convergence, but the evidence is confounded by rendering-variant dependence and fixture concentration.

4. **No realised-outcome/ROI test was used to rescue a source-quality failure.**
   The preregistration made outcome analysis secondary and optional. Once the primary source-quality gate failed decisively, no post-hoc result mining was justified.

Final decision:

> **B01 WITH CURRENT BETEXPLORER `reference_proxy` = NOT VALIDATED / DOWNGRADE.**

This does not prove that same-time cross-market residuals never exist. It shows that this BetExplorer rendering stream is not a sufficiently stable reference source for the intended B01 claim.

No BUY/PASS, true-EV, profitability, stake, or live-betting conclusion is supported.

## 7. Variant source-quality gate

All 57 matched fixture-selection pairs were seen under more than one declared BetExplorer variant.

Independent audit:

- fixture-selection pairs with median residual sign flip across variants: **10 / 57**
- fixture-selection pairs with any positive residual: **14**
- positive fixture-selection pairs positive in every observed variant: **2 / 14**
- positive fixture-selection pairs whose positivity disappears in at least one variant: **12 / 14**

This is the strongest falsification evidence.

The B01 residual should represent a same-time cross-market price difference, not a property of which BetExplorer rendering variant happened to be served. The observed dependence is too strong to support that interpretation.

No preferred variant was selected after seeing results, per preregistration.

## 13. Final disposition

### What is established

- Exact same-round Sporttery-vs-reference_proxy comparisons are feasible.
- The prospective dataset contains positive proxy residual observations.
- Some longer-horizon reference movement reduces those residuals.

### What is not established

- That BetExplorer provides a stable enough rendering-independent reference probability for B01.
- That positive residual rows represent true positive EV.
- That the high-threshold tail generalises beyond a handful of fixtures.
- That any profitable live betting rule exists.

### Decision

> **B01 WITH CURRENT BETEXPLORER REFERENCE_PROXY = NOT VALIDATED / DOWNGRADE.**

The correct next scientific move is **not** threshold tuning, team/league filtering, or a backtest over these rows.

If B01 is revived, the reopen condition should be a materially better reference source: preferably named, timestamped, and stable across requests/renderings.
