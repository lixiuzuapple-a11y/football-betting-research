Not validated for target Sporttery fixtures.
4. German [OpenLigaDB](https://github.com/OpenLigaDB/OpenLigaDB-Samples): public unauthenticated structured match results API. First entry matchID=77256 for Bayern Munich in 2025/26 Bundesliga round 1. Metadata can be user-maintained and not official settlement source.
