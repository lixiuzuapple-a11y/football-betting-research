## 7. Research finding

Mechanically, the primary stale+executable definition fires frequently:

- 438 rows;
- 17 fixtures;
- 3 calendar days.

But this is not clean D01-B evidence.

The decisive observation is:

> **580/580 matched reference changes occurred in batches of more than 20 simultaneously changed reference events.**

Therefore:

- batch=1 matched evidence: 0;
- batch<=5 matched evidence: 0;
- 6–20 matched evidence: 0;
- >20 matched evidence: all 580.

The entire matched sample is dominated by mass synchronized BetExplorer page changes.

In addition:

- 178/580 matched changes revert to the prior tuple at the next same-variant observation;
- among primary candidates, 170/438 revert on the next same-variant observation;
- exact batch sizes repeatedly cluster around fixed large values such as 31, 35, 56, 42 and 65.

These facts do not prove a specific hidden BetExplorer rendering mechanism, but they are incompatible with treating this sample as a clean set of isolated sharp-market information arrivals.

## 8. Economic attribution

The mechanical current proxy-EV gate produces:

- >0: 49 rows / 7 fixtures;
- >=2%: 20 / 5;
- >=5%: 3 / 3;
- >=10%: 2 / 2.

All are in >20-event batches.

More importantly, reconstruction of the pre-move proxy EV shows:

- 50 positive post-move outcome instances;
- 48 were already positive before the reference move;
- only 2 crossed from <=0 to >0 because of the move.

The two zero-crossing examples were themselves weak D01-B evidence:

- batch sizes 98 and 47;
- honest reference-change brackets about 4.21 h and 1.08 h.

Therefore the apparently large current proxy-EV examples are overwhelmingly pre-existing cross-market residuals, not value attributable to the D01-B move.

Those residuals belong to the separate B01 question.

## 9. Timing interpretation

The Sporttery confirmation mechanism is technically strong:

- median confirmation delay after the BetExplorer response: about 0.20 s;
- 95th percentile: about 0.36 s.

The problem is not confirmation latency.

The reference side is the weak link:

- primary median comparable-observation bracket ~62.5 s;
- 95th percentile ~64.6 min;
- maximum ~9.9 h;
- large synchronized batches dominate.

Thus the current `reference_proxy` does not supply the clean timing semantics D01-B needs.
